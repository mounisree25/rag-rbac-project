Employee Benefits Guide (HR - Internal)

All full-time employees are eligible for health, dental, and vision coverage
starting on day one. TechNova matches 401(k) contributions up to 4%.
Employees receive 20 PTO days per year plus 10 company holidays. Parental
leave is 16 weeks paid for all parents, regardless of gender. Reach out to
hr@technova.example for enrollment questions.
