Q3 Financial Summary (Finance - Confidential)

Q3 revenue reached $6.2M, up 18% quarter-over-quarter, driven by enterprise
tier upgrades. Gross margin improved to 71%. Customer acquisition cost (CAC)
fell to $4,100 due to the new outbound SDR program. The board has approved
an additional $2M marketing budget for Q4 aimed at the EU expansion.
